# PulseIQ Dashboard Layout Design

## 1. Header
- Dashboard title: PulseIQ — Headcount & Departments
- Subtitle: Workforce Overview Dashboard

## 2. Four KPI Cards
1. Total Employees — 745
2. Total Departments - 11
3. Average Tenure - 4.55 years
4. New Hires - 10

## 3. Charts Below the KPI Cards
- Department-wise Employee Count
- Headcount Trend
- Workforce by Role
- Joiners vs Leavers

Every chart must have a clear title and a one-line takeaway.

## 4. Side Filters
- Department
- Location
- Employment Type
- Date Range

## 5. Visual Design
- Use a colour-blind-friendly palette.
- Use readable fonts and clear labels.
- Keep the layout clean and consistent.
- Show two chart columns on laptop screens.
- Stack charts vertically on mobile screens.
- Make filters collapsible on smaller screens.

## 6. Design Review
- Place Total Employees first because it is the key workforce metric.
- Use sufficient contrast and avoid relying on colour alone.
- Keep chart titles and labels readable.
- Use actual data for KPI values; do not invent missing values.

## 7. Review Changes
- Prioritised Total Employees at the top.
- Grouped KPI cards above charts.
- Placed filters in a separate side panel.
- Planned a responsive layout for laptop and mobile.

## Dashboard Takeaways

- **Total Employees:** The warehouse contains 745 unique employees.
- **Total Departments:** Employees are distributed across 11 departments.
- **Average Tenure:** Current employees have an average tenure of 4.55 years.
- **New Hires:** The dashboard reports 10 new hires for the selected period.
- **Department-wise Employee Count:** The chart highlights differences in employee distribution across departments.

*Note: Department-wise and hiring insights should be refined after validating the final chart values and date filters.*
